About this book

THE MASTERY OF BEING: The Secret Universal Laws of Reality, Spirit, Manifestation, Attraction & Reality Creation - William Walker Atkinson.

THE SECRET CAUSE OF SUCCESS: The Hidden Law of Being That Creates Reality!

The Mastery of Being by William Walker Atkinson is one of the most profound explorations of reality, consciousness, and spiritual causation within the New Thought tradition. In this episode of The Secrets of Success & Wealth Podcast, we go beyond surface-level manifestation teachings and enter a deeper metaphysical framework—where reality is not something you live in, but something you continuously express through being.Unlike many modern interpretations of the Law of Attraction that focus primarily on desire, visualization, or affirmation, Atkinson’s work shifts the foundation entirely. It proposes that everything begins with Being itself—a unified field of Spirit, Mind, and Manifestation. What you experience as “external reality” is not separate from you, but a direct expression of the underlying intelligence that you already are. This episode breaks down each part and chapter of the book in a structured, practical, and deeply philosophical way, so you can understand how consciousness shapes existence at its core.

INTRODUCTION: THE CORE IDEA OF THE MASTERY OF BEINGAtkinson presents a radical premise: there is only one Reality, and everything else is appearance. That Reality is not material—it is spiritual, intelligent, and creative. Human beings are not separate observers trying to influence life from the outside; they are individualized expressions of the same universal Spirit.From this perspective, the Law of Attraction is not merely a psychological technique—it is a reflection of a deeper ontological truth: you attract according to what you are, not what you want. This shifts the focus from effort and striving to alignment, awareness, and identity.The book is structured into three major parts:Part I: RealityPart II: SpiritPart III: ManifestationEach section builds upon the previous one, guiding the reader from intellectual understanding to spiritual realization and finally to practical creative expression.

1. The Quest for Truth: The journey begins with questioning appearances. Atkinson emphasizes that most people accept surface reality without inquiry, mistaking sensory experience for ultimate truth. This chapter introduces the seeker’s path: the recognition that truth must be discovered inwardly, not externally validated. The “quest” is not about collecting information, but dissolving illusion.
