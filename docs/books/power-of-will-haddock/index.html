Episode description

THE POWER OF WILL: Master Your Mind, Strengthen Your Character, and Develop the Inner Force That Creates Success – Frank Channing Haddock (1907).

In this powerful episode of the Secrets of Success & Wealth Podcast, we explore one of the most practical and influential classics of personal development ever written: The Power of Will by Frank Channing Haddock. Long before modern self-help, neuroscience, and peak-performance psychology, Haddock taught that the true source of achievement, wealth, influence, health, and personal mastery lies in the development of one hidden faculty: the will. According to Haddock, success is not primarily a matter of talent, luck, education, or circumstances. It is the result of a trained will directing the mind, body, and actions toward definite purposes. This remarkable book serves as both a philosophy of success and a practical training manual. Haddock demonstrates how every individual can strengthen determination, improve concentration, gain control over emotions, develop greater self-discipline, and awaken latent powers through systematic practice. The lessons presented here remain as relevant today as when they were first published more than a century ago.

PART I — THE WILL AND SUCCESS

1. The Will and Its Action

The journey begins with an exploration of the nature of the will itself. Haddock explains what the will is, how it operates, and why it serves as the central governing force of human life. He shows that every action, achievement, habit, and decision ultimately arises from the exercise of willpower. Readers discover that strengthening the will is not merely desirable—it is essential for anyone seeking success and self-mastery.

2. Tests of Will

How strong is your will? In this chapter, Haddock introduces practical methods for measuring the strength and effectiveness of one's determination. He reveals the signs of a disciplined will and exposes the hidden weaknesses that prevent many people from accomplishing their goals. These tests provide valuable insight into personal character and self-control.

3. The Conduct of Life

Success depends upon more than isolated acts of determination. It requires a consistent approach to living. Haddock examines the relationship between willpower, habits, decisions, ethics, and daily conduct. He explains how the will shapes character and how character ultimately determines destiny.

4. Diseases of the Will

One of the most fascinating sections of the book explores the various weaknesses that undermine personal power. Procrastination, indecision, laziness, fear, impulsiveness, inconsistency, and lack of persistence are examined as "diseases" of the will. Haddock offers practical remedies for overcoming these destructive tendencies and replacing them with strength and purpose.

5. Training of the Will

Can willpower be developed? Haddock answers with a resounding yes. This chapter introduces systematic methods for strengthening determination through conscious effort, disciplined action, and mental training. Readers learn that the will grows stronger through exercise, just as muscles develop through physical training.

6. Training of the Will, Continued: A Study of Moods

Many people become prisoners of their emotional states. Haddock explains how moods influence decisions, behavior, productivity, and success. More importantly, he demonstrates how a trained will can rise above temporary emotions and maintain control regardless of circumstances. This chapter provides timeless wisdom for developing emotional resilience and mental stability.

7. Some General Rules

The first part concludes with a collection of practical principles for daily life. Haddock summarizes the most important lessons for cultivating determination, focus, self-command, persistence, and effective action. These rules provide a blueprint for strengthening personal power in every area of life.

PART II — THE WILL AND SENSE-CULTURE:

In the second half of the book, Haddock introduces a unique and often overlooked idea: the development of willpower through the systematic training of the senses. He argues that strengthening perception improves concentration, attention, self-control, and mental efficiency.

8. Suggestions for Practice

Before beginning specific exercises, Haddock outlines the principles that govern successful training. He explains how consistent practice develops mental discipline and prepares the individual for greater mastery over both mind and body.

9. Exercises for the Eye

Vision is one of humanity's most important senses. Through a series of practical exercises, Haddock teaches readers how to improve observation, concentration, visual memory, and attention to detail. These exercises help cultivate mental focus while strengthening the will's ability to direct awareness.

10. Exercises for the Ear

Listening is a skill rarely developed consciously. This chapter presents techniques for improving auditory perception, concentration, and sensitivity to sound. By learning to listen more carefully, readers also learn to strengthen attention and mental discipline.

11. Exercises in Taste

Haddock turns to one of the least appreciated senses and demonstrates how conscious attention to taste can increase self-control and sensory awareness. The exercises encourage mindfulness, discipline, and greater mastery over impulsive habits.

12. Exercises in Smell

Often ignored in everyday life, the sense of smell becomes a tool for developing concentration and awareness. Haddock shows how deliberate sensory training sharpens perception and strengthens the individual's ability to focus attention at will.

13. Exercises in Touch

Touch connects us directly to the physical world. Through specialized exercises, readers learn to increase sensitivity, awareness, and concentration while improving the mind's ability to remain attentive and controlled.

14. Exercises for the Nerves

The nervous system plays a critical role in energy, endurance, and emotional balance. Haddock provides methods for cultivating calmness, stability, and resilience. These practices help individuals maintain composure under pressure and develop greater control over their reactions.

15. Exercises for the Hands

The hands are instruments of both action and expression. This chapter explores exercises designed to improve coordination, precision, steadiness, and conscious control. Haddock demonstrates how physical discipline contributes directly to mental discipline.

16. Exercises in Steadiness

Steadiness is one of the defining characteristics of a powerful will. Through practical exercises involving posture, movement, attention, and control, readers learn to cultivate patience, persistence, and unwavering concentration. The lessons extend far beyond physical steadiness into every area of life and achievement.

17. General Health

The book concludes by emphasizing the importance of physical well-being. Haddock explains that a strong will functions most effectively when supported by a healthy body. He discusses the relationship between vitality, energy, mental clarity, and success, showing how health serves as a foundation for personal power and achievement.

More than a century after its publication, The Power of Will remains one of the most practical guides ever written on self-mastery and achievement. Its teachings anticipate many modern discoveries in psychology, habit formation, performance science, and personal development. Whether your goal is financial success, stronger discipline, improved concentration, better decision-making, emotional control, leadership, or personal growth, Haddock's timeless wisdom offers a clear path forward. He reminds us that greatness is not reserved for a gifted few—it is built through the daily exercise of will, purpose, and disciplined action. Join us as we uncover this forgotten masterpiece and learn how to develop the inner force that drives success, wealth, character, health, and lasting achievement.
