About this book

THE POWER OF WILL: Master Your Mind, Strengthen Your Character, and Develop the Inner Force That Creates Success – Frank Channing Haddock (1907).

In this powerful episode of the Secrets of Success & Wealth Podcast, we explore one of the most practical and influential classics of personal development ever written: The Power of Will by Frank Channing Haddock. Long before modern self-help, neuroscience, and peak-performance psychology, Haddock taught that the true source of achievement, wealth, influence, health, and personal mastery lies in the development of one hidden faculty: the will. According to Haddock, success is not primarily a matter of talent, luck, education, or circumstances. It is the result of a trained will directing the mind, body, and actions toward definite purposes.This remarkable book serves as both a philosophy of success and a practical training manual. Haddock demonstrates how every individual can strengthen determination, improve concentration, gain control over emotions, develop greater self-discipline, and awaken latent powers through systematic practice. The lessons presented here remain as relevant today as when they were first published more than a century ago.

PART I — THE WILL AND SUCCESS1. The Will and Its ActionThe journey begins with an exploration of the nature of the will itself. Haddock explains what the will is, how it operates, and why it serves as the central governing force of human life. He shows that every action, achievement, habit, and decision ultimately arises from the exercise of willpower. Readers discover that strengthening the will is not merely desirable—it is essential for anyone seeking success and self-mastery.

2. Tests of WillHow strong is your will? In this chapter, Haddock introduces practical methods for measuring the strength and effectiveness of one's determination. He reveals the signs of a disciplined will and exposes the hidden weaknesses that prevent many people from accomplishing their goals. These tests provide valuable insight into personal character and self-control.

3. The Conduct of LifeSuccess depends upon more than isolated acts of determination. It requires a consistent approach to living. Haddock examines the relationship between willpower, habits, decisions, ethics, and daily conduct. He explains how the will shapes character and how character ultimately determines destiny.
