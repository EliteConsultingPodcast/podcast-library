Summary

Robert Collier's The Secret of the Ages (1926) sets out in seven volumes his view that the mind is the ultimate creative power and that anyone can tap it to achieve their desires.

Collier discusses the subconscious as 'the genie of your mind', the role of desire, visualisation, faith and gratitude, and how these apply to health, money and relationships. He blends New Thought metaphysics with the direct style of the advertising copywriter he was.

The book stresses that clear desire, a definite mental image and confident expectation, followed by action, are the 'secret' behind every achievement.

Key ideas

  • Desire is the starting point of creation
  • The subconscious works on clear mental images
  • Expect the result with faith and gratitude
  • Follow inspiration with action
