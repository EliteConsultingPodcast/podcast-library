About this book

The Majesty of Calmness: A Path to Influence, Success, and True Happiness - By William George Jordan (1898) - HQ Full Book.

William George Jordan’s The Majesty of Calmness is a profound and inspiring work that explores the virtues of serenity, self-mastery, and personal influence. Written in a direct and eloquent style, this book serves as a guide to cultivating inner peace, developing strong character, and achieving personal success. Jordan presents timeless wisdom on self-reliance, resilience, and the importance of calmness in navigating life’s challenges. His words encourage readers to rise above anxiety, impatience, and failure by embracing a composed and purposeful existence. Each chapter of the book tackles a specific aspect of character development, providing insightful reflections on how individuals can transform their lives by fostering inner tranquility and strength. Jordan's message remains relevant today, urging readers to resist the frantic pace of modern life and instead cultivate patience, dignity, and unwavering focus.

1. The Majesty of CalmnessIn this opening chapter, Jordan underscores the supreme power of calmness. He describes it as the hallmark of true strength and a fundamental quality of great leaders and thinkers. He argues that serenity is not mere passivity but a disciplined state of mind that enables individuals to respond to life’s trials with wisdom and confidence. Calmness, he explains, is a force that commands respect and admiration, allowing people to maintain clarity and control in even the most challenging situations.

2. Hurry, the Scourge of AmericaJordan critiques the relentless pace of modern life, which he views as a destructive force that leads to anxiety, inefficiency, and superficial thinking. He warns that hurry is the enemy of calmness and deep reflection, eroding both health and happiness. By advocating for a more deliberate and thoughtful approach to work and life, he encourages readers to resist the temptation to rush, emphasizing that true success comes from steady, focused effort rather than frantic speed.

3. The Power of Personal InfluenceThis chapter explores the unseen yet profound effect that individuals have on others. Jordan explains that personal influence is not about coercion or persuasion but rather the natural impact of one's character and presence. He highlights the importance of integrity, sincerity, and positive energy, asserting that those who cultivate these qualities will naturally inspire and uplift those around them. The chapter serves as a reminder that personal influence is a force for good when wielded with wisdom and authenticity.
