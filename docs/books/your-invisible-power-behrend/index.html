YOUR INVISIBLE POWER: Unlocking the Law of Attraction and the Power of Your Mind - Genevieve Behrend (1921) - HQ Full Book.

"Your Invisible Power" by Genevieve Behrend is a timeless guide to understanding the power of the mind and its ability to shape our reality. First published in 1921, the book is a masterclass on the principles of mental science, visualizations, and the law of attraction, drawing from the teachings of Thomas Troward, one of the foremost pioneers in the field of mental science. Through Behrend's clear explanations and practical advice, readers learn to harness their thoughts and imagination to manifest desires and achieve personal success. The book focuses on the importance of mental imagery, affirmations, and belief in the unseen forces that govern our lives. It emphasizes the importance of faith, visualization, and the words we speak as vital components of personal transformation and growth.

Chapters:

1. Order of Visualization:

In the opening chapter, Behrend stresses the importance of developing a systematic approach to visualization. She discusses how the mind's creative power is directed through clear and organized mental images. The chapter outlines a structured process that helps individuals focus their desires, creating a mental picture that is vivid and powerful enough to influence reality. She suggests that the mind works best when it is given specific, clear instructions, and that each visualization should be approached with intention and belief.

2. How to Attract to Yourself the Things You Desire:

This chapter explains the connection between thought and attraction. Behrend discusses how individuals can manifest their desires by cultivating the right mental attitude and focusing on positive, constructive thoughts. She introduces the idea that thoughts act as magnets, drawing corresponding situations, people, and circumstances into one’s life. The chapter teaches how to align one’s desires with mental images that are powerful enough to attract the desired outcomes, emphasizing the role of belief and trust in the process.

3. Relation Between Mental and Physical Form:

Behrend explores the interplay between mental energy and physical reality. She explains that mental images have the ability to shape physical circumstances, demonstrating how the invisible world of thoughts influences the visible world of material form. The chapter explains the concept that our external circumstances are a reflection of our inner thoughts, beliefs, and visualizations. By altering our mental landscape, we can bring about changes in our physical environment.

4. Operation of Your Mental Picture-Desire:

Building on the previous chapter, Behrend elaborates on how the mental picture operates as a driving force behind manifestation. She explains how to make this mental picture a living, dynamic force that propels individuals toward their desires. The chapter outlines steps to activate the mental picture and work with it until the desired outcome becomes reality. She emphasizes the role of repetition and emotional involvement in the process of manifestation.

5. Expressions from Beginners:

In this chapter, Behrend shares the experiences and expressions of individuals who have just begun practicing mental science and visualization techniques. She highlights common mistakes, misconceptions, and the challenges newcomers face. This section provides encouragement and reassurance to those just starting, showing them that while the process may take time, persistence and faith will lead to success.

6.Suggestions for Making Your Mental Picture:

This chapter offers practical suggestions on how to craft and refine the mental images that will drive manifestation. Behrend outlines techniques to ensure that mental pictures are clear, detailed, and aligned with one’s desires. She discusses the importance of vivid imagery, emotional connection, and regular reinforcement to strengthen these mental pictures and enhance their power.

7. Things to Remember:

A concise chapter that summarizes key concepts from the book, Behrend offers a series of reminders for the reader. She reiterates the importance of clarity, focus, and unwavering belief in the power of the mind. The chapter emphasizes that success in mental science requires both patience and persistence, and that one’s thoughts and beliefs are always shaping reality, whether consciously or unconsciously.

8. Why I Took Up the Study of Mental Science:

In this personal reflection, Behrend shares her own journey into the study of mental science. She explains how she discovered the power of the mind and why she chose to pursue it further. Her story serves as an inspiration to readers, showing how mental science transformed her life and opened doors of opportunity she hadn’t imagined before.

9. How I Attracted to Myself Twenty Thousand Dollars:

This chapter presents a personal account of how Behrend used the principles of mental science to manifest a significant sum of money. She breaks down her process, offering a detailed look at how she visualized her financial goals and how those visualizations eventually led to tangible results. This chapter serves as a real-world example of how visualization and belief can bring about financial abundance.

10. How I Became the Only Personal Pupil of T. Troward, the Great Mental Scientist:

Behrend shares the story of how she became the personal student of Thomas Troward, one of the most influential mental scientists of the time. She explains how her own faith and commitment to the principles of mental science caught the attention of Troward, and how this relationship changed the course of her life. This chapter illustrates the importance of perseverance and dedication in the study of mental science.

11. How to Bring the Power in Your Word Into Action:

In this chapter, Behrend emphasizes the transformative power of words. She explains how words are a direct expression of thought and can be used to bring about change in one’s life. By speaking affirmations with belief and conviction, individuals can direct their energy and focus toward manifesting their desires. The chapter teaches how to align spoken words with inner thoughts to activate the forces of creation.

12. How to Increase Your Faith:

This chapter discusses how to cultivate and strengthen faith in the power of the mind and the universe. Behrend explains that faith is a muscle that can be developed through practice and affirmation. The chapter offers practical exercises to help readers increase their faith in their ability to create their reality, and how this faith is essential to achieving success.

13. The Reward of Increased Faith:

Once faith is strengthened, Behrend argues, the rewards are profound. This chapter outlines the benefits of increased faith, including greater mental clarity, emotional resilience, and the ability to manifest desires more quickly. Behrend illustrates how faith is the key that unlocks the power of the mind and enables individuals to achieve their goals.

14. How to Make Nature Respond to You:

Here, Behrend explores the concept that nature, or the universe, responds to our mental commands. She explains that by aligning our thoughts with the laws of nature and mental science, we can influence the events and circumstances in our lives. The chapter teaches how to work in harmony with the natural laws of attraction, abundance, and creation.

15. Faith with Works - What It Has Accomplished:

This chapter discusses the importance of taking action alongside faith. Behrend explains that while belief is crucial, it must be coupled with effort and practical steps toward achieving one’s desires. The chapter highlights the synergy between faith and action, and how this combination accelerates the manifestation process.

16. Suggestions as to How to Pray or Ask, Believing You Have Already Received:

Behrend provides detailed instructions on how to ask for what you want with full belief that it has already been received. She emphasizes the importance of praying or affirming as though the desired outcome is already a reality, which helps align the subconscious mind with the conscious desire. This chapter teaches how to cultivate a mindset of abundance and trust.

17. Things to Remember:

The final chapter reiterates essential principles for success in mental science. Behrend encourages readers to remember the power of their thoughts, words, and faith in the process of creating their reality. She reminds readers that persistence, belief, and emotional involvement are the keys to transforming their dreams into reality.

Through Your Invisible Power, Genevieve Behrend provides a practical yet profound roadmap for anyone seeking to unlock the hidden potential of their mind and manifest their desires. This work remains a foundational text in the fields of mental science and the law of attraction.
