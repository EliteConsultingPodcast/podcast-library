Summary

The Kybalion, published in 1908 under the pseudonym 'Three Initiates' (widely attributed to William Walker Atkinson), presents a modern interpretation of Hermetic philosophy.

It is structured around seven principles: Mentalism (the All is mind), Correspondence (as above, so below), Vibration, Polarity, Rhythm, Cause and Effect, and Gender. Each is explained as a universal law operating on physical, mental and spiritual planes.

The book then describes 'mental transmutation', the idea that by understanding these laws a person can change mental states, for example by shifting from one pole of a feeling to its opposite. It has strongly influenced modern esoteric and self-help writing.

Key ideas

  • Seven Hermetic principles describe how reality works
  • Everything vibrates and has its opposite pole
  • Understanding rhythm helps one stay balanced
  • Mental states can be deliberately transmuted
