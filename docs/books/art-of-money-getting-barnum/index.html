Summary

P. T. Barnum, the showman and entrepreneur, published The Art of Money Getting in 1880 as a set of practical rules for earning and keeping money.

His advice includes choosing the right vocation, avoiding debt, persevering, doing work thoroughly, using the best tools, advertising, being polite and honest, and not scattering one's efforts. He warns repeatedly against extravagance and living beyond one's means.

The tone is humorous and anecdotal, but the rules are sound and timeless: earn honestly, spend less than you earn and build a reputation for reliability.

Key ideas

  • Choose work that suits you
  • Avoid debt and live below your means
  • Advertise and be reliable
  • Persevere and finish what you start
