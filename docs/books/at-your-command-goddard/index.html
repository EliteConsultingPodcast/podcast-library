Episode description

"At Your Command" by Neville Goddard is a profound exploration of the power of the human mind and its ability to shape reality. Published in the mid-20th century, this timeless work delves into the transformative principles of positive thinking and the law of assumption. Neville Goddard, a influential figure in the New Thought movement, guides readers through the concept that our thoughts and beliefs create the fabric of our experiences.

The book emphasizes the idea that individuals have the innate capacity to command their destinies through the conscious use of imagination. Neville Goddard draws on metaphysical concepts, biblical references, and personal anecdotes to illustrate how the mind can be harnessed to manifest desires and reshape circumstances. Whether aspiring for success, health, or happiness, "At Your Command" serves as a practical guide to unlocking one's potential and living a more intentional life.

Neville Goddard's eloquent prose and compelling insights inspire readers to recognize their own creative power and take command of their destinies. Rooted in the belief that imagination is the key to realizing dreams, this classic work continues to captivate and empower individuals on their journey to self-discovery and manifestation.
