Summary

The Empire of Business (1902) collects Andrew Carnegie's essays and addresses on business, money and economics.

Carnegie advises young men on the road to success, explains money and currency, discusses the relationship between labour and capital, and comments on railways, steel and trade. His tone is practical and confident.

The essays show how Carnegie connected individual effort with national prosperity, and they contain his recurring advice: concentrate, save, aim high and be thorough.

Key ideas

  • Concentrate your energy on one field
  • Aim high and take responsibility
  • Understand money and economics
  • Labour and capital share common interests
