Summary

Success Habits collects material from Napoleon Hill's lectures of the early 1950s, in which he explained his philosophy to live audiences in a direct, conversational style. It reads as a practical guide to turning principles into habits.

Hill walks through definiteness of purpose, the Master Mind, applied faith, going the extra mile, a pleasing personality, accurate thinking, self-discipline and the related principles of his system, giving examples of how each one operates in ordinary careers.

The underlying message is that success comes from repeated behaviour. By deliberately practising these principles until they become automatic, a person reshapes both character and circumstances.

Key ideas

  • Habits, not occasional effort, create success
  • Clarify your purpose and master it daily
  • Accurate thinking separates facts from opinion
  • Self-discipline governs emotion and action
