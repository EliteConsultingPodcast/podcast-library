Summary

Florence Scovel Shinn's 1925 book presents life as a game of giving and receiving governed by spiritual law. Drawing on her work as a metaphysical teacher in New York, she argues that whatever we send out in word or deed returns to us.

Shinn explains the law of prosperity, the power of the spoken word, non-resistance, forgiveness, intuition and the importance of faith. Each chapter is illustrated with short anecdotes from her clients.

Her central tool is the affirmation: short, positive statements used to replace fear with confidence. The book remains a classic of New Thought because of its simple, practical tone.

Key ideas

  • What you give out returns to you
  • Words and affirmations shape experience
  • Non-resistance and forgiveness free energy
  • Follow intuition and act with faith

THE GAME OF LIFE & HOW TO PLAY IT: Unlock the Secrets to Prosperity and Success - Florence Scovel Shinn (1925).

The Game of Life and How to Play It by Florence Scovel Shinn is a timeless self-help classic, first published in 1925, that blends practical wisdom with spiritual insights. Shinn, a metaphysical teacher and artist, presents life as a game and emphasizes that individuals can unlock its full potential by understanding and applying the spiritual laws that govern it. Through a series of practical steps and spiritual principles, Shinn guides readers in achieving prosperity, success, and peace of mind, while creating a harmonious and fulfilling life. The book is divided into several chapters, each focusing on a specific spiritual law or principle. Below is an overview of each chapter with a short description.

1. The Game of Life

In this opening chapter, Florence Scovel Shinn introduces the concept of life as a game, where individuals are players who can influence the outcome through their thoughts, words, and actions. Shinn explains that life is not a random series of events but a game governed by spiritual laws, and to win, one must learn to play by these laws. She encourages readers to think of themselves as champions in the game of life, believing in their own worth and power to manifest their desires. Shinn underscores the importance of a positive attitude, visualizing success, and maintaining faith that life is working in their favor.

2. The Law of Prosperity

Shinn delves into the concept of prosperity, explaining that it is not limited to wealth alone, but encompasses all areas of life, including health, relationships, and peace of mind. She emphasizes that prosperity is a natural state that can be realized by aligning one’s thoughts with abundance and rejecting thoughts of lack or limitation. The Law of Prosperity teaches readers to believe in their infinite supply of good, as it is their divine inheritance. Shinn encourages the practice of gratitude, affirmations, and visualization to invite prosperity into one's life.

3. The Power of the Word

This chapter focuses on the creative power of words. Shinn explains that words are not merely sounds; they have a powerful influence on the material world. According to the Law of the Word, speaking with intention and purpose is a key to shaping one's reality. Shinn explains that every word spoken is either a creative force or a destructive force, so it is essential to speak positively and in alignment with one’s desires. She teaches the importance of using affirmations and avoiding negative or limiting language.

4. The Law of Nonresistance

Shinn introduces the Law of Nonresistance, which emphasizes the power of letting go and not resisting negative situations or emotions. She teaches that resistance to life’s challenges only magnifies them, whereas acceptance and nonresistance allow for smooth navigation through difficult circumstances. By embracing the law of nonresistance, individuals can release their struggles and trust that the universe will handle their problems, bringing solutions in unexpected and harmonious ways.

5. The Law of Karma and the Law of Forgiveness

In this chapter, Shinn explains the relationship between the Law of Karma and the importance of forgiveness. Karma, according to Shinn, refers to the idea that every action has consequences, and those consequences are a reflection of one's thoughts and deeds. She emphasizes the need to forgive others, not for their benefit, but for one's own spiritual growth. Holding onto grudges only perpetuates negative karma, whereas forgiveness clears the path for peace, healing, and spiritual progress. Shinn teaches that releasing resentment is essential for achieving freedom and prosperity.

6. Casting the Burden

In this chapter, Shinn elaborates on the practice of "casting the burden," which involves releasing the weight of worry, fear, and guilt. When individuals carry burdens of stress or negative emotions, they hinder their ability to attract positive experiences. Shinn teaches that by letting go of these burdens, individuals free themselves to receive abundance, peace, and joy. She offers practical exercises to help readers let go of limiting beliefs and emotional baggage, which in turn opens them to greater success and fulfillment.

7. Love

Shinn focuses on the transformative power of love in this chapter. She explains that love is a universal force that heals, attracts, and uplifts. By cultivating love within oneself and toward others, individuals can create harmony in their lives. Love is not limited to romantic relationships; it is an attitude that can be applied to every aspect of life, including work, health, and social connections. Shinn teaches that love is the antidote to fear, lack, and negativity, and it is essential for manifesting a prosperous life.

8. Intuition or Guidance

Shinn introduces the concept of intuition as the inner guidance system that leads individuals toward their highest good. She explains that everyone has an intuitive sense or “still small voice” that, when trusted, can guide them to make wise decisions and navigate through life’s challenges. By learning to trust and follow their intuition, individuals can make decisions that align with their divine purpose and attract success. Shinn encourages the practice of quieting the mind through meditation and prayer to strengthen one’s connection with inner guidance.

9. Perfect Self-Expression or the Divine Design

In this chapter, Shinn discusses the importance of aligning with one’s divine design or purpose. She explains that everyone has unique talents, skills, and gifts that are meant to be expressed in the world. By discovering and living in accordance with one’s true purpose, individuals can achieve fulfillment and success. Shinn emphasizes that true self-expression is not about striving for approval or recognition but about expressing the highest and best version of oneself in the world. By doing so, individuals align with their divine blueprint and achieve their highest potential.

10. Denials and Affirmations

This chapter focuses on the practical use of denials and affirmations as tools for transforming one’s life. Shinn explains that denials are statements that negate negative thoughts or beliefs, while affirmations are positive statements that reinforce desired outcomes. By denying negative beliefs and affirming positive truths, individuals can reprogram their minds and align their thinking with their desires. Shinn provides a series of examples of both denials and affirmations that readers can use to shift their mindset and attract prosperity, health, and success.

11. Denials and Affirmations (continued)

In the continuation of the previous chapter, Shinn goes deeper into the power of denials and affirmations. She emphasizes that the key to their effectiveness lies in emotional conviction and faith. It is not enough to merely repeat affirmations; they must be spoken with belief and a sense of certainty. Shinn also explains that by consistently practicing denials and affirmations, individuals can transform negative patterns of thought and behavior, opening the door to greater peace and prosperity.

The Game of Life and How to Play It offers a practical and spiritual roadmap for living a life filled with abundance, peace, and success. Florence Scovel Shinn’s teachings are grounded in metaphysical principles and emphasize the power of the mind, words, and emotions in shaping reality. Through the application of the spiritual laws discussed in each chapter, readers can achieve a deeper understanding of themselves and their potential, unlocking the path to a more fulfilling and prosperous life.
