Summary

A companion to The Science of Getting Rich, Wallace D. Wattles' 1910 book applies the same approach to personal greatness rather than wealth.

Wattles argues that every person has access to a universal intelligence and can develop power by thinking independently, acting with integrity and seeing others as equals. He stresses consistent thought, service and acting on one's best judgement in each moment.

The book outlines practical exercises in thinking and conduct, presenting greatness as the gradual result of aligning daily actions with one's highest understanding.

Key ideas

  • Greatness is available to anyone who develops it
  • Think for yourself and act on your best judgement
  • See and treat others as equals
  • Consistent daily action builds personal power

THE SCIENCE OF BEING GREAT: The Timeless Blueprint for Personal Power and Success - Wallace D. Wattles (1910).

"The Science of Being Great" by Wallace D. Wattles, published in 1910, is a seminal work in the field of self-improvement and personal development. This book builds upon the principles laid out in Wattles' earlier work, *The Science of Getting Rich*, and serves as a guide to achieving greatness in one’s life. The author emphasizes that greatness is not an inherent trait but a result of understanding and applying specific mental and spiritual principles. Wattles offers a practical approach to achieving personal greatness, advocating for a mindset that fosters positive thinking, creative visualization, and a commitment to self-improvement.

Overview of the Book

Wattles presents a systematic approach to achieving greatness, emphasizing that every individual has the potential to rise to greatness if they adhere to certain principles. The book is divided into several chapters, each focusing on different aspects of personal development, self-mastery, and the cultivation of a great mindset. Wattles draws on philosophical concepts and practical advice, combining metaphysical ideas with actionable steps that readers can take to transform their lives.

Key Themes and Ideas

1. The Nature of Greatness

In the opening chapters, Wattles defines what he means by "greatness." He argues that greatness is not just about wealth or power but involves the ability to influence and uplift others. Wattles contends that everyone has the potential to be great, and the first step toward greatness is recognizing this potential within oneself. He emphasizes the importance of self-belief and the idea that one's thoughts and attitudes shape their reality.

2. The Power of Thought

One of the core themes of Wattles' work is the power of thought. He asserts that thoughts are a creative force that can shape one's experiences and outcomes. Wattles encourages readers to cultivate positive thoughts and focus on their goals, believing that doing so will attract the circumstances necessary for achieving greatness. He discusses the importance of visualization and mental imagery, urging readers to envision their desired future clearly and consistently.

3. The Importance of Personal Responsibility

Wattles stresses the need for personal responsibility in achieving greatness. He argues that individuals must take ownership of their lives and the choices they make. This chapter emphasizes the importance of understanding that one's current circumstances are the result of past thoughts and actions. To create a better future, individuals must adopt a proactive approach, making conscious choices that align with their goals.

4. The Role of Faith and Belief

Wattles delves into the role of faith and belief in the journey toward greatness. He asserts that having faith in oneself and the universe is crucial for overcoming obstacles and challenges. Wattles encourages readers to develop an unwavering belief in their abilities and the positive outcomes they desire. This chapter explores the concept of "faith in action," emphasizing that belief must be accompanied by purposeful action to manifest desired results.

5. The Science of Creation

In this chapter, Wattles discusses the principles of creation and how individuals can harness these principles to achieve greatness. He introduces the idea that the universe is abundant and that by aligning one's thoughts and actions with the laws of the universe, one can create their reality. Wattles encourages readers to focus on abundance rather than scarcity, fostering a mindset that attracts opportunities for growth and success.

6. The Practice of Gratitude

Wattles highlights the importance of gratitude as a vital practice in the pursuit of greatness. He argues that expressing gratitude for what one has fosters a positive mindset and attracts more blessings. This chapter outlines practical ways to cultivate gratitude in daily life, emphasizing that a grateful heart opens the door to greater abundance and fulfillment.

7. The Development of Personal Character

In this section, Wattles emphasizes the importance of character in achieving greatness. He argues that true greatness is rooted in strong moral principles, integrity, and a commitment to personal growth. Wattles encourages readers to cultivate qualities such as honesty, kindness, and generosity, as these attributes not only contribute to personal greatness but also positively impact others.

8. The Impact of Greatness on Society

Wattles discusses the broader impact of individual greatness on society as a whole. He believes that when individuals strive for greatness, they contribute to the upliftment of others and the betterment of humanity. This chapter emphasizes the interconnectedness of individuals and society, highlighting that personal greatness has the potential to inspire and empower others to pursue their paths of greatness.

9. The Importance of Continuous Learning

Wattles advocates for the importance of continuous learning and self-improvement in the journey toward greatness. He encourages readers to seek knowledge, develop new skills, and expand their horizons. This chapter emphasizes that greatness is a lifelong pursuit that requires ongoing growth and adaptation to changing circumstances.

10. The Role of Service in Greatness

In the final chapters, Wattles discusses the significance of service and contribution to others in the pursuit of greatness. He asserts that true greatness involves serving others and making a positive impact on their lives. This chapter encourages readers to adopt a mindset of service, viewing their success as intertwined with the well-being of others. By helping others achieve their goals, individuals can enhance their path to personal greatness.

Conclusion: The Legacy of "The Science of Being Great"

Wallace D. Wattles’ *The Science of Being Great* serves as a timeless guide for anyone seeking to unlock their potential and achieve greatness. His emphasis on the power of thought, personal responsibility, faith, and service resonates with readers across generations. The book combines practical advice with profound philosophical insights, offering a roadmap for individuals to cultivate a mindset that fosters success and fulfillment.

Wattles’ teachings encourage readers to believe in their abilities, visualize their goals, and take deliberate actions toward their desired outcomes. His advocacy for gratitude, character development, and continuous learning provides a holistic approach to personal growth.

As a precursor to modern self-help literature, The Science of Being Great continues to inspire individuals to pursue their dreams and make a positive impact on the world. Wattles’ legacy lives on through the countless individuals who have embraced his principles, striving for greatness in their lives and contributing to the greater good of society. Ultimately, this book serves as a powerful reminder that greatness is not reserved for a select few but is attainable for anyone willing to embrace the principles of personal development and service to others.
