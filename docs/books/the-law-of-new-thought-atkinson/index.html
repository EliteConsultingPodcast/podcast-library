Episode description

THE LAW OF NEW THOUGHT : Mastering Mental Power and the Law of Attraction for Success – A Study of Fundamental Principles and Their Application - William Walker Atkinson (1902).

In this episode of The Secret of Success Podcast, we explore William Walker Atkinson’s seminal 1902 work, The Law of New Thought: A Study of Fundamental Principles and Their Application. As one of the foundational texts of the New Thought movement, this book provides a comprehensive roadmap for understanding the creative power of thought, the interconnection of mind, body, and spirit, and the ethical and practical applications of consciousness in shaping personal destiny.

Atkinson, a pioneering figure in the New Thought movement, presents a methodical framework for harnessing mental and spiritual faculties to manifest desired outcomes, cultivate self-mastery, and contribute positively to the world. Far from mere wishful thinking, Atkinson’s approach combines philosophy, metaphysics, and practical psychology to reveal how deliberate thought and alignment with universal principles can transform every aspect of life. This episode breaks down each of the 16 chapters, unpacking Atkinson’s timeless insights, connecting them to modern law-of-attraction practices, and providing listeners with actionable strategies for cultivating positive thought patterns, visualization, and conscious intention. Whether you are exploring spiritual awakening, personal growth, or manifestation, this guide illuminates the path toward mastery of self and alignment with universal laws.

Chapter 1: What is the ‘New Thought’?

Atkinson introduces the New Thought movement, defining it as a philosophy based on the creative power of mind and the principle that thought is the primary agent shaping reality. He explains that New Thought is not bound to a single religion or dogma but is a universal philosophy connecting mind, spirit, and action. The chapter lays the groundwork for understanding how conscious mental effort can influence life circumstances, setting the stage for the practical applications presented in later chapters.

Chapter 2: Thoughts are Things

This chapter articulates one of the central axioms of New Thought: thoughts are not abstract or inert; they carry real energy capable of influencing external conditions. Atkinson demonstrates how focused, constructive thoughts create tangible results, while negative or scattered thoughts may manifest obstacles. Practical exercises in cultivating awareness of thought patterns and deliberately directing mental energy are introduced, illustrating how personal reality can be shaped consciously.

Chapter 3: The Law of Attraction

Atkinson explores the principle that like attracts like. He explains how thoughts and emotions emit vibrational energy that draws corresponding circumstances and individuals into one’s life. By aligning mental focus with positive intentions and constructive imagery, one can magnetize success, health, and prosperity. The chapter also addresses common pitfalls—such as doubts, fear, or contradictory desires—that can weaken attraction power, offering strategies to maintain mental and emotional alignment.

Chapter 4: Mind Building

Building a strong and resilient mind is essential for conscious creation. Atkinson provides techniques for cultivating mental clarity, concentration, and emotional balance. He emphasizes discipline, self-observation, and the deliberate practice of constructive thinking as methods for developing a mind capable of sustaining high-level focus and manifesting desired outcomes. Listeners learn that mental growth parallels the expansion of conscious influence over life circumstances.

Chapter 5: The Dweller of the Threshold

Here, Atkinson examines the inner obstacles that resist spiritual and personal growth—the fears, doubts, and unresolved patterns he terms the “Dweller of the Threshold.” Confronting these inner limitations is necessary for progression. He offers strategies for self-awareness, emotional purification, and the cultivation of courage, demonstrating how overcoming internal resistance allows the mind to align more fully with higher principles and creative power.

Chapter 6: Mind and Body

Atkinson highlights the profound connection between mental and physical states. Thoughts, emotions, and attitudes directly influence health, vitality, and overall well-being. By cultivating positive thought, emotional equilibrium, and disciplined mental practices, individuals can enhance physical functioning and manifest more harmonious life experiences. This chapter bridges metaphysical theory with tangible health benefits, reinforcing the practical power of New Thought.

Chapter 7: The Mind and Its Planes

The human mind operates on multiple levels, each with its own capacities and limitations. Atkinson delineates the conscious, subconscious, and higher mental planes, explaining their functions and interrelations. Understanding these planes allows the practitioner to direct mental energy more efficiently, engage the subconscious creatively, and access higher faculties for problem-solving, inspiration, and manifestation.

Chapter 8: The Sub-conscious Plane

Delving deeper into the subconscious, Atkinson describes it as the repository of habits, memories, and automatic functions. Properly trained and directed, the subconscious serves as an engine for achieving goals effortlessly. Techniques for programming the subconscious—through affirmation, visualization, and repetition—are detailed, showing listeners how to transform habitual mental patterns into constructive forces aligned with their intentions.

Chapter 9: The Super-conscious Faculties

Beyond the subconscious lies the superconscious: the higher intelligence capable of insight, intuition, and creative inspiration. Atkinson explains how developing connection with the superconscious provides guidance, expands awareness, and facilitates extraordinary creative problem-solving. Practices for attuning to this plane, including meditation, contemplation, and conscious receptivity, are outlined as tools for personal and spiritual evolution.

Chapter 10: The Soul’s Question

This chapter explores the deeper purpose of existence and the intrinsic questions posed by the soul: Who am I? Why am I here? Atkinson invites reflection on personal mission, spiritual alignment, and the pursuit of higher ideals. By engaging with these questions consciously, individuals clarify goals, refine intentions, and align thought patterns with long-term spiritual and material success.

Chapter 11: The Absolute

Atkinson introduces the concept of the Absolute—the infinite, universal consciousness underlying all existence. Understanding the Absolute provides context for the power of thought and the interconnectedness of life. Listeners learn that aligning personal will with universal principles magnifies creative ability, enhances harmony, and fosters both spiritual and material fulfillment.

Chapter 12: The Oneness of All

Here, the emphasis is on the interconnected nature of life. Atkinson shows that all beings and circumstances are expressions of the same universal intelligence. Recognition of this oneness cultivates empathy, altruism, and ethical action, while also reinforcing the law of attraction: thoughts directed toward the good of others and the harmony of life amplify personal success and spiritual growth.

Chapter 13: The Immortality of the Soul

Atkinson discusses the enduring nature of consciousness beyond physical life, emphasizing the continuity of growth, experience, and learning. Understanding the soul’s immortality encourages broader perspective, reduces fear of failure or loss, and deepens commitment to ethical living and the cultivation of thought as a creative and transformative force.

Chapter 14: The Unfoldment

Personal development is a gradual process of unfoldment, where latent potential is brought into active expression. Atkinson describes how disciplined mental practices, conscious alignment, and ethical action gradually reveal higher faculties and abilities. This chapter highlights patience, persistence, and faith as essential components of conscious growth and the manifestation of one’s highest potential.

Chapter 15: The Growth of Consciousness

Consciousness is dynamic, capable of expansion through deliberate effort. Atkinson explores methods for broadening awareness, increasing perception, and refining thought. Growth of consciousness enables clearer decision-making, heightened creativity, and more effective alignment with universal laws, resulting in enhanced personal and spiritual efficacy.

Chapter 16: The Soul’s Awakening

The final chapter culminates in the awakening of the soul to its full creative and spiritual potential. Atkinson describes awakening as the harmonious integration of mind, body, and spirit, resulting in mastery over thought, alignment with higher principles, and the ability to consciously shape one’s life. This chapter serves as both inspiration and practical guide, encouraging ongoing practice and reflection to maintain mental clarity, emotional balance, and spiritual purpose.

The Law of the New Thought remains a foundational guide for anyone seeking mastery of mind, manifestation of desires, and alignment with universal principles. Atkinson’s systematic approach shows that thought is not inert but a dynamic force capable of shaping life circumstances, health, and spiritual development. Through conscious application, disciplined mental practice, and alignment with ethical and universal principles, individuals can cultivate a life of abundance, fulfillment, and continuous growth.
