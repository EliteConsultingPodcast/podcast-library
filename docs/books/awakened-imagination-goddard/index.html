About this book

AWAKENED IMAGINATION: How to Create Wealth, Success, and Your Ideal Life - Neville Goddard (1954).

The power which makes the achievement of aims – the attainment of desires – inevitable.

Awakened Imagination is one of Neville Goddard’s most profound and transformative works, offering a powerful interpretation of the creative force within every human being. At its core, this book teaches that imagination is not merely a mental faculty for daydreaming or fantasy—it is the very power of creation itself. Neville presents imagination as divine, the hidden identity of God within man, and the key to shaping reality consciously.This work is both philosophical and deeply practical. Through clear explanations, symbolic interpretations, and real-life applications, Neville reveals how individuals can move from passive observers of life to deliberate creators. Each chapter builds upon the last, guiding the reader to awaken to their true nature and apply the “law” of imagination to transform their world.Below is a detailed exploration of each chapter and its significance.

Chapter 1: Who Is Your Imagination?Neville begins by confronting a fundamental question: What is imagination? Most people view imagination as something secondary—an escape from reality. Neville overturns this assumption entirely. He declares that imagination is the true identity of man, the divine presence within.Drawing from biblical symbolism, he equates imagination with God Himself, suggesting that the human imagination is not separate from the divine but is its very expression. This means that every act of imagining is an act of creation.The chapter challenges the reader to shift their self-concept. Instead of identifying with the body or external conditions, Neville urges the reader to identify as imagination itself—the unseen cause behind all visible effects. This realization is the foundation of all conscious creation. If imagination is God, then imagining becomes a sacred and powerful act.

Chapter 2: Sealed InstructionsIn this chapter, Neville explores the idea that truth has always been available but hidden in symbolic form. Sacred texts, particularly the Bible, are described as “sealed instructions”—not meant to be taken literally, but interpreted psychologically.He explains that many stories in scripture are allegories of inner processes. When understood correctly, they reveal how imagination works and how reality is formed. The “sealing” refers to the fact that these truths remain inaccessible until one is ready to see beyond surface meanings.Neville emphasizes that awakening imagination requires insight and reinterpretation. The instructions are already within reach, but they must be “unsealed” through awareness. This chapter encourages readers to look beyond appearances and discover deeper meanings in both scripture and their own experiences.
