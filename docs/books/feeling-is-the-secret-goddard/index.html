Feeling is the Secret

by Neville Goddard · 1 episode · full audiobook reading

▶ Listen now

Summary

Neville Goddard's short 1944 book explains his central teaching: the feeling attached to an idea is what impresses it on the subconscious and brings it into experience.

Goddard discusses the law and its operation, the importance of the state before sleep, prayer as the feeling of the wish fulfilled, and the role of the spirit. He urges readers to assume the feeling of already having what they desire.

The book is concise and practical, and it is often recommended as the best introduction to Neville's work.

Key ideas

  • Feeling impresses ideas on the subconscious
  • Assume the feeling of the wish fulfilled
  • Use the drowsy state before sleep
  • Control your feelings to control your experience
