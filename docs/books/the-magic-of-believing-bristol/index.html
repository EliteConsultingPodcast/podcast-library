Summary

Claude M. Bristol, a journalist and businessman, wrote The Magic of Believing in 1948 to explain the power of belief that he had observed in successful people and in his own life.

Bristol describes how repeated suggestion shapes the subconscious, and offers practical techniques: writing goals on cards, using mirrors to strengthen self-confidence, visualising outcomes and surrounding oneself with supportive influences.

He presents belief as a practical tool rather than a mystical idea: what we deeply believe we tend to act on, and our actions shape the results we see.

Key ideas

  • Belief drives action and results
  • Repetition plants ideas in the subconscious
  • Use cards and mirror technique to reinforce goals
  • Choose your influences carefully
