Summary

Charles F. Haanel's The New Psychology (1924) continues the ideas of his Master Key System in a set of essays on the creative power of thought.

Haanel explains the psychology of success, the law of abundance, the Master Mind, the law of attraction and the relationship between the conscious, subconscious and universal mind. He links these ideas to health and business.

The book argues that understanding mental laws allows a person to direct thought deliberately and thereby shape circumstances.

Key ideas

  • Thought creates conditions
  • Abundance follows from right thinking
  • The Master Mind connects individuals to greater power
  • Understanding mental law enables control

Episode description

THE NEW PSYCHOLOGY: How Thought Creates Reality, Wealth & Success - Charles Haanel.

Master your thoughts, align with universal laws, and consciously create wealth, success & personal power.

Charles Haanel, an influential figure in the early 20th-century self-help and metaphysical movement, developed a philosophy centered on harnessing the power of thought to shape reality. His work, "The New Psychology", is a comprehensive exploration of the mind's power, offering deep insights into how individuals can use mental faculties to achieve success, abundance, health, and well-being. The book integrates aspects of metaphysics, psychology, and philosophy, presenting a holistic framework for personal and spiritual growth.

The chapters of "The New Psychology" each focus on different principles and laws governing human thought and its influence on the world. Below is a breakdown of the key themes of each chapter:

1. The Psychology of Success

This introductory chapter explores the mental attitudes and behaviors that distinguish successful individuals from others. Haanel emphasizes the importance of a success-oriented mindset, suggesting that the primary factor in success is not external conditions but internal states of mind. By cultivating mental discipline, focus, and determination, individuals can set themselves on a path to success, regardless of their starting point.

2. The Law of Abundance

Here, Haanel delves into the concept of abundance, which he defines as a natural state of the universe. The chapter explains that abundance is not merely about financial wealth but encompasses all areas of life, including health, relationships, and personal fulfillment. According to Haanel, thoughts of scarcity block the flow of abundance, while a mindset rooted in gratitude and expectation opens the door to receiving life's gifts.

3. The Master Mind

The concept of the "Master Mind" refers to the collective power of group consciousness or the combined mental energy of individuals working together towards a common goal. Haanel explains how this principle works to exponentially amplify the effectiveness of individuals by harmonizing their thoughts and efforts. He encourages readers to seek out like-minded individuals to collaborate and generate powerful creative forces.

4. The Law of Attraction

One of the most popular and influential concepts in Haanel's philosophy is the Law of Attraction, which posits that like attracts like. Our thoughts, emotions, and beliefs act as a magnetic force, drawing into our lives people, circumstances, and outcomes that are in harmony with our dominant mental state. By consciously focusing on positive, empowering thoughts, individuals can attract success, happiness, and prosperity.

5. The Universal Mind

In this chapter, Haanel introduces the concept of the Universal Mind, a limitless source of intelligence, creativity, and power that pervades the entire universe. He asserts that all individuals are connected to this Universal Mind and can tap into its infinite potential. The key to accessing this power is through alignment with universal principles, such as truth, harmony, and balance.

6. The Conscious Mind

Haanel defines the conscious mind as the seat of rational thought, decision-making, and awareness. It is through the conscious mind that individuals can choose their thoughts and direct their attention toward desired outcomes. However, Haanel also warns that the conscious mind can be easily distracted and influenced by external circumstances, making it essential to develop focus and discipline.

7. The Creative Process

This chapter focuses on the mechanics of creation, emphasizing the role of thought as the primary creative force in the universe. Haanel explains how the creative process works in stages, beginning with an idea or desire, which is then developed through visualization and emotional investment. By holding a clear mental image of the desired outcome and sustaining it with belief and expectation, individuals can bring their goals to fruition.

8. Vibration

Vibration is a fundamental principle in Haanel's philosophy, representing the underlying energy that animates all matter and thought. Everything in the universe vibrates at different frequencies, and thoughts are no exception. By understanding the principle of vibration, individuals can consciously raise their mental and emotional frequencies to align with their desired outcomes.

9. Causation

Haanel discusses the law of causation, which states that every effect has a cause, and every cause produces an effect. In the realm of thought, this means that our beliefs and attitudes are the cause of the conditions we experience in life. By changing the underlying mental causes, individuals can alter the effects in their external world, leading to positive transformations in all areas of life.

10. Equilibrium

Equilibrium refers to the balance between opposing forces in the universe. Haanel emphasizes the importance of maintaining mental and emotional equilibrium to achieve harmony and success. When individuals are balanced and centered, they are more capable of responding to challenges effectively and making wise decisions.

11. Physiology

In this chapter, Haanel explores the relationship between the mind and the body, arguing that mental states have a profound impact on physical health. Positive thoughts and emotions can enhance well-being and vitality, while negative mental states can lead to illness and disease. The mind and body are interconnected, and by cultivating a healthy mental attitude, individuals can promote physical health.

12. The Psychology of Medicine

Haanel delves deeper into the mind-body connection, specifically focusing on the psychological aspects of medicine. He suggests that many illnesses are psychosomatic in origin, meaning they are caused or exacerbated by mental and emotional factors. By addressing the underlying psychological causes, individuals can often experience healing without the need for invasive medical interventions.

13. Mental Chemistry

This chapter discusses the concept of mental chemistry, which refers to the way thoughts and emotions interact to produce specific outcomes. Haanel likens the mind to a chemical laboratory, where different mental states can combine to create either positive or negative effects. By mastering mental chemistry, individuals can learn to control their emotional responses and create more favorable conditions in their lives.

14. Mental Medicine

Mental medicine builds on the previous chapters, offering practical techniques for using the mind to heal the body. Haanel provides methods for harnessing the power of suggestion, visualization, and affirmation to promote physical health and well-being. He emphasizes the importance of belief in the healing process and encourages readers to cultivate a positive mental attitude towards health.

15. Orthobiosis

Orthobiosis is a term used by Haanel to describe the correct way of living in harmony with natural laws. This chapter discusses the principles of right thinking, right action, and right living, which lead to a balanced and fulfilling life. Haanel argues that by aligning with universal laws, individuals can achieve not only personal success but also contribute to the greater good of society.

16. Biochemistry

Biochemistry is examined from a metaphysical perspective, with Haanel explaining how mental states influence the chemical processes in the body. He suggests that thoughts can affect the body's biochemistry, leading to either health or illness depending on the quality of the thoughts.

17. The New Psychology

The titular chapter presents the core principles of Haanel’s “new psychology,” which integrates modern psychological theories with ancient metaphysical teachings. The new psychology emphasizes the power of thought as a creative force and offers practical techniques for harnessing mental energy to achieve desired outcomes.

18. Suggestion

Suggestion is a powerful tool for influencing the subconscious mind. In this chapter, Haanel discusses how individuals can use suggestion to reprogram their beliefs and attitudes, leading to positive changes in behavior and circumstances.

19. Psycho-Analysis

Haanel briefly touches on psychoanalysis, focusing on how understanding the subconscious mind can lead to deeper self-awareness and personal growth. He explains how repressed thoughts and emotions can manifest in negative patterns, and how bringing these to conscious awareness can facilitate healing.

20. Metaphysics

This chapter delves into metaphysical principles, exploring the nature of reality, consciousness, and the universe. Haanel explains that the physical world is a reflection of the mental and spiritual realms, and by mastering metaphysical laws, individuals can transcend limitations and create their desired reality.

21. Philosophy

In the final chapter, Haanel reflects on philosophical questions related to existence, purpose, and the nature of the human mind. He encourages readers to adopt a philosophical approach to life, seeking wisdom and understanding through introspection and the study of universal truths.

Through these chapters, The New Psychology serves as a comprehensive guide to mastering the mind and unlocking human potential, blending scientific, psychological, and spiritual perspectives into a coherent philosophy of success.
