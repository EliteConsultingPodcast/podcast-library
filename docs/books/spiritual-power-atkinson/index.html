SPIRITUAL POWER - THE INFINITE FOUNT: Mastering Spiritual Energy - William Walker Atkinson (1922) - HQ Full Book.

Dive into the profound wisdom of "Spiritual Power - The Infinite Fount" by William Walker Atkinson, a cornerstone of New Thought philosophy published in 1922. This podcast episode from "Secrets of Success" explores Atkinson's transformative teachings, revealing how spiritual power serves as an boundless inner reservoir that aligns perfectly with the principles of attraction and manifestation. Atkinson, a trailblazing author in self-help and occult literature, co-wrote this volume (part of his "Personal Power" series) with Edward E. Beals, emphasizing that true power stems not from external forces but from the immanent spirit within each person. In an era when modern psychology and spirituality were converging, Atkinson demystifies the human connection to the divine, showing how harnessing this infinite fount can attract abundance, health, and fulfillment.

At its core, the book posits that spiritual power is the ultimate creative force, akin to the Law of Attraction's vibrational energy. Atkinson argues that by aligning one's will with this universal spirit, individuals can consciously shape their reality. He draws from ancient philosophies, Eastern mysticism, and emerging scientific ideas about energy and matter, blending them into practical guidance. Unlike rigid religious doctrines, Atkinson's approach is inclusive, inviting listeners to awaken their innate potential through introspection, willpower, and attunement to the cosmos. This episode unpacks how these ideas prefigure modern manifestation techniques, such as visualization and positive affirmation, by rooting them in spiritual essence rather than mere mental tricks.

Listeners will discover how Atkinson's insights challenge materialistic views, urging a shift toward recognizing spirit as the animating principle of life. He warns against the pitfalls of ego-driven pursuits, instead advocating for a harmonious union with infinite power. Through real-world analogies—like the flow of electricity or the growth of a seed—Atkinson illustrates how spiritual energy operates subtly yet potently, attracting desired outcomes when channeled with focused intent. This work's enduring relevance lies in its empowerment message: in a world of chaos, tapping the infinite fount fosters resilience, creativity, and peace. As the podcast delves deeper, you'll learn exercises for daily practice, making abstract concepts actionable for attracting love, success, and spiritual growth.

The episode structures its exploration around the book's eight chapters, each building on the last to guide seekers from foundational inquiry to cosmic unity. Below is a short description of each chapter, highlighting key teachings and their ties to the Law of Attraction.

1. The Quest for Truth

This opening chapter ignites the intellectual journey by examining humanity's eternal search for the underlying reality of existence. Atkinson critiques the limitations of pure intellect, which relies on sensory data, and introduces intuition as a higher faculty for perceiving truth. He contrasts materialism's focus on physical causes with non-materialism's emphasis on spiritual will-power as the source of all energy. Through philosophical realism, he argues that truth emerges when intellect and intuition unite, leading to personal illumination. In Law of Attraction terms, this quest encourages aligning thoughts with universal truth to manifest desires, warning that doubt blocks the flow of spiritual power. Readers are urged to question assumptions, fostering a mindset that attracts clarity and empowerment.

2. Material Substance

Here, Atkinson delves into the role of matter as the foundational canvas of the universe, animated by immanent spirit. He rejects idealism's denial of material reality, asserting that substance provides stability amid constant change, much like the ether in ancient philosophies. Scientific concepts, from atoms to electrons, are woven in to show matter's responsiveness to spiritual influence. The chapter emphasizes that material forms are "ensouled" by spirit, creating the cosmos through law and order. For attraction practitioners, this means viewing physical manifestations as extensions of inner spirit—by infusing intention into material goals, one attracts tangible results. Atkinson teaches that denying matter's reality hinders manifestation, advocating gratitude for the physical world as a bridge to infinite power.

3. Actuating Energy

Atkinson identifies actuating energy as the dynamic force driving cosmic change, equating it to spiritual power rather than blind mechanical force. He explores energy, power, and force as volitional expressions of spirit, governed by cause and effect. Materialism's "pure force" is dismissed as lifeless, while human will-power serves as a model for understanding universal energy. This energy unifies the universe into an interdependent whole, manifesting creation and evolution. In the context of the Law of Attraction, it underscores that focused will activates vibrational energy to draw in experiences. The chapter inspires listeners to recognize their inner energy as a creative tool, using it to overcome inertia and attract positive transformations through deliberate action and belief.

4. Immanent Spirit

This chapter defines immanent spirit as the indwelling essence permeating all existence—ethereal, active, and intrinsic. Tracing the word "spirit" to its roots in breath and vitality, Atkinson outlines its manifestations: presence (as ultimate reality), life (sentience and volition), and power (strength and force). Known through self-consciousness and intuition, spirit transcends material limits. He contrasts it with external deities, emphasizing its accessibility within. For Law of Attraction enthusiasts, this immanence means the universe responds to internal alignment; by attuning to this spirit, one becomes a co-creator, attracting harmony. Practical advice includes meditative reflection to sense this presence, fostering a vibrational match for desired realities and dissolving feelings of separation.

5. Spirit: Essential Life

Focusing on spirit's life aspect, Atkinson portrays it as the vital force infusing sentience, conation (desire-driven effort), and volition across the cosmos. Life is more fundamental than consciousness, expressed in everything from atomic vibrations to human will. Modern science's hints at universal livingness align with ancient views of spirit animating all. Sentience enables perception, conation fuels motivation, and volition empowers choice. In attraction principles, this essential life means vitality attracts vitality—cultivating inner aliveness draws health and abundance. The chapter urges introspection to experience this life-force, warning that disconnection leads to stagnation, while embrace manifests vibrant experiences through joyful, purposeful living.

6. Spirit: Essential Power

Atkinson examines spirit as infinite power, manifesting as strength, energy, and force—all rooted in will-power. He argues that true power is conscious and volitional, not mechanical, with examples like plant growth illustrating life's dynamic force. Contrasting materialism's blind energy, he posits spiritual power as self-renewing and guiding cosmic motion. Personal will-power, discovered through self-analysis, mirrors this essence. For the Law of Attraction, this power is the engine of manifestation; strengthening will aligns vibrations to attract goals. The chapter provides exercises for building inner strength, emphasizing that recognizing one's core power dissolves limitations, enabling effortless drawing of opportunities and success.

7. Creator and Creation

This chapter views the universe as an eternal creative evolution, with spirit as the creator energizing material substance into forms. Atkinson critiques anthropomorphic gods, favoring vitalism where spirit expresses life, mind, and will through cycles of becoming. The will-to-live actuates preservation and growth, with immanence making spirit present in all. God is the core of spirit, echoed in "I Am That I Am." In attraction terms, creation mirrors personal manifestation—individuals as focal points co-create by attuning to this process. The teachings encourage seeing life as purposeful, attracting fulfillment by participating in cosmic creativity and embracing unity with the divine source.

8. Unison with Infinity

Culminating the book, this chapter guides achieving harmony with the infinite spirit, realizing one's identity as part of the macrocosm. Through the "Message of Truth," Atkinson affirms individuals are spirit using matter as tools, drawing infinite power via attunement. Methods include silence, contemplation, and faith to commune with the divine. Immanence bridges finite and infinite, fostering empowerment in daily life. For Law of Attraction, this unison amplifies manifestation; tuning like a receiver attracts boundless resources. The chapter inspires transcendent awareness, transforming challenges into opportunities and leading to self-mastery, enlightenment, and perpetual attraction of peace and abundance.

In summary, this episode of "The Secrets of Success" revives Atkinson's masterpiece as a blueprint for spiritual empowerment, blending timeless philosophy with practical tools for modern seekers. By exploring these chapters, listeners unlock the infinite fount, aligning with universal laws to manifest their highest potential. Whether you're new to spirituality or a seasoned practitioner, this work offers profound shifts, reminding us that true attraction begins within.
