Episode description

WILL POWER: The Hidden Dynamic Force Behind Success and Achievement - William Walker Atkinson (1922).

What if the greatest force for success, wealth, and achievement has been hidden within you all along? Before modern self-help, one book revealed the secret force behind focus, discipline, achievement, and unstoppable success.

In the Secrets of Success Podcast, we uncover timeless principles that empower individuals to unlock their full potential and achieve extraordinary results. Today, we dive into a foundational gem from the New Thought era: "Will Power - Your Dynamic Forces" by William Walker Atkinson, originally published in 1922. Atkinson, a pioneering figure in self-help and mental science, was a lawyer, merchant, and prolific author who penned over 100 books under various names. Influenced by Eastern philosophies, psychology, and occult traditions, he emphasized the mind's role in shaping reality.

This book, the fifth volume in his "Personal Power" series, positions will power as the ultimate engine of human achievement—a vibrant, dynamic energy that drives success, overcomes adversity, and transforms dreams into reality. Atkinson argues that will is not an innate gift reserved for the elite but a trainable force accessible to all. Through clear, practical insights, he demystifies the will, breaking it into stages and offering exercises to cultivate it. In an age of distractions and quick fixes, this 1922 masterpiece remains relevant, reminding us that true success stems from inner mastery.

Whether you're an entrepreneur battling setbacks, a student seeking focus, or anyone striving for personal growth, Atkinson's teachings provide a roadmap to harness your "dynamic forces" for unstoppable momentum. Join us as we explore each chapter, revealing how will power can propel you toward your goals.

Chapter 1: Will Power

This opening chapter introduces will power as the core dynamic force behind all human accomplishment, portraying it as an active energy rather than a passive trait. Atkinson explains that will is the "master self" directing thoughts, emotions, and actions toward desired outcomes, drawing on examples from history and everyday life to illustrate its transformative potential. He provides initial techniques, such as visualization and affirmation, to awaken and strengthen this force, emphasizing that anyone can develop it through conscious effort. By the end, readers are inspired to view will power as their personal superpower for conquering challenges and manifesting success.

Chapter 2: The Nature of Will

Here, Atkinson delves into the fundamental essence of will, defining it as a mental faculty rooted in consciousness and distinct from mere desire or instinct. He explores its psychological and philosophical underpinnings, describing will as a bridge between thought and action, influenced by factors like attention and motivation. The chapter clarifies common misconceptions, such as confusing will with stubbornness, and outlines its innate qualities, urging readers to recognize it as a neutral energy that can be directed positively or negatively. Through analogies from nature and science, Atkinson sets the stage for deeper cultivation, encouraging self-reflection to understand one's own will nature.

Chapter 3: Conative Will

Focusing on the "conative" aspect, this chapter examines the will's role in initiating effort and striving toward goals, likening it to the impulse that propels movement in the physical world. Atkinson describes conative will as the raw drive for action, essential for overcoming inertia and laziness, and shares strategies to amplify it, including building habits through repeated small efforts. He warns against undirected conation, which leads to wasted energy, and illustrates with case studies of successful individuals who harnessed this force to persist amid failures. Ultimately, it empowers readers to ignite their inner motivation for sustained progress.

Chapter 4: Deliberative Will

Atkinson shifts to the deliberative phase, where will involves careful consideration, weighing options, and planning before action. This chapter highlights the importance of rational analysis in decision-making, preventing impulsive errors and aligning choices with long-term objectives. He offers exercises like pros-and-cons lists and meditative reflection to sharpen this faculty, drawing parallels to strategic thinkers in business and warfare. By mastering deliberative will, readers learn to balance emotion with logic, transforming potential chaos into orderly advancement toward success.

Chapter 5: Determinative Will

This section explores determinative will as the decisive force that commits to a course of action, solidifying intentions and exerting control over circumstances. Atkinson portrays it as the "turning point" where ideas become unbreakable resolutions, using metaphors of forging steel to convey its unyielding strength. Practical advice includes affirmations and mental rehearsals to reinforce determination, with warnings about vacillation that weakens resolve. The chapter inspires by showing how determinative will has fueled historical triumphs, equipping readers to make firm choices that shape their destiny.

Chapter 6: Active Will

Building on prior stages, active will is presented as the execution phase, where determination manifests in tangible deeds and persistent follow-through. Atkinson stresses the need for disciplined application, sharing techniques like time-blocking and accountability to convert plans into results. He addresses common barriers like procrastination and fatigue, offering mindset shifts to maintain momentum. This chapter underscores that true power lies in doing, not just intending, motivating readers to embody their will in daily actions for real-world victories.

Chapter 7: Will-Training

A hands-on guide, this chapter provides a systematic program for developing will power through progressive exercises, akin to physical training for muscles. Atkinson outlines drills such as resisting minor temptations, focusing attention for extended periods, and practicing self-command in routine tasks. He emphasizes gradual progression to avoid burnout, incorporating relaxation techniques to balance effort. By treating will as a skill to be honed, the chapter empowers readers to build resilience and confidence, turning weakness into strength for lifelong success.

Chapter 8: Will Consciousness

Atkinson introduces the concept of "will consciousness," urging readers to cultivate heightened awareness of their inner will as a living, vibrant entity. This chapter explores self-observation methods to recognize will in operation, fostering a deeper connection that amplifies its potency. He discusses how conscious attunement leads to intuitive guidance and greater self-mastery, with exercises like journaling will experiences. The result is a profound shift, where will becomes an ever-present ally in navigating life's complexities.

Chapter 9: Will-Atmosphere

In the concluding chapter, Atkinson describes creating a "will-atmosphere"—an aura of focused energy that influences both self and surroundings. He explains how thoughts and attitudes generate a magnetic field that attracts opportunities and repels negativity, offering visualization practices to build this protective sphere. The chapter ties everything together, showing how a strong will-atmosphere inspires others and sustains personal growth. It leaves readers with a call to action: radiate your dynamic forces to create a life of abundance and fulfillment.

As we wrap up this exploration on the Secrets of Success Podcast, "Will Power - Your Dynamic Forces" stands as a beacon for anyone seeking to reclaim control in a chaotic world. Atkinson's 1922 insights, blending psychology, spirituality, and practicality, remind us that success isn't luck—it's the deliberate unleashing of our inner will. Apply these principles, and watch your life transform. Tune in next time for more secrets that propel you forward.
