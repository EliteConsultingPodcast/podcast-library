HENRY FORD - TODAY & TOMORROW: Henry Ford’s Blueprint for Industrial Prosperity, Human Progress & the Future of Wealth - Henry Ford (1926).

In this powerful episode of the Secrets of Success & Wealth Podcast, we explore Today and Tomorrow by Henry Ford—a bold and visionary sequel to his earlier work My Life and Work. Written in collaboration with Samuel Crowther, this 1926 masterpiece goes far beyond autobiography. It lays out a complete philosophy of industry, economics, and human advancement—one that remains strikingly relevant in today’s age of automation, globalization, and technological disruption.

Ford does not merely explain how he built an empire; he reveals why it worked. His central idea is radical in its simplicity: true wealth is created through service, efficiency, and the elimination of waste—not through speculation, exploitation, or artificial scarcity. At the heart of his philosophy lies the “wage motive,” a system where high wages, low prices, and continuous innovation create a self-sustaining cycle of prosperity.

From the roaring furnaces of the River Rouge complex to the quiet potential of decentralized village industries, Ford paints a vivid picture of a world where machines liberate humanity rather than replace it, where business serves society, and where progress is measured not by profit alone—but by the well-being of all.

This episode breaks down every chapter into clear, practical insights, translating Ford’s industrial philosophy into timeless principles for success, wealth creation, and personal mastery.
