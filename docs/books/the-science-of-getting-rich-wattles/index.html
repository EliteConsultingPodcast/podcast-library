Summary

Wallace D. Wattles' 1910 classic sets out to describe a 'science' of becoming wealthy, arguing that getting rich results from thinking and acting in a 'certain way' rather than from environment, talent or luck.

Wattles proposes that a formless substance underlies all things and responds to clear, persistent mental images held with faith and gratitude. He insists that this must be paired with efficient action in one's present work, giving more in use value than one takes in money.

A distinctive theme is his rejection of competition: Wattles urges readers to be creative rather than competitive and to leave everyone they deal with feeling better off. The book later influenced the modern 'law of attraction' movement.

Key ideas

  • Form a clear mental picture of what you want
  • Hold it with faith and gratitude
  • Act efficiently every day in your current position
  • Create rather than compete; give more value than you take

THE SCIENCE OF GETTING RICH: How to Use the Law of Attraction for Wealth and Success - Wallace D. Wattles (1910).

The Science of Getting Rich by Wallace D. Wattles, first published in 1910, is one of the earliest and most influential works that blends philosophy with financial success. Its central theme is that wealth is available to all who understand and apply the principles of science, focused thought, and effective action. Wattles firmly believed that there was a “science” behind getting rich, and this science could be taught to anyone, irrespective of their current financial standing. The book combines self-help advice with a practical, step-by-step approach to manifesting wealth and abundance. Below is a brief overview of each chapter to help you understand the core principles discussed by Wattles.

1. The Right to Be Rich

Wattles begins by asserting that everyone has the right to be rich. He argues that there is nothing wrong with desiring wealth and that poverty is not a virtue. He suggests that wealth enables individuals to lead more fulfilling, prosperous lives and that it is their divine right to attain it. Wattles emphasizes that wealth does not just provide material benefits, but it also allows for a better and more meaningful life, enabling individuals to contribute more to society.

2. There is a Science of Getting Rich

This chapter introduces the concept of the "Science of Getting Rich," which Wattles argues is based on universal laws of nature. He presents the idea that wealth is governed by laws, just as physical phenomena like gravity are. The book is not about random luck or personal talent; instead, Wattles asserts that there are specific principles that anyone can follow to acquire wealth. By following these principles, individuals can align themselves with the natural laws of abundance.

3. Is Opportunity Monopolized?

Wattles challenges the idea that wealth is only for a select few. He emphasizes that opportunities for getting rich are not monopolized by the elite or certain classes. Instead, the universe offers abundant opportunities to everyone. The key is recognizing and acting on these opportunities, regardless of societal constraints or your current position. Wattles argues that anyone, anywhere, can attain wealth if they understand and apply the science behind it.

4. The First Principle in the Science of Getting Rich

The first principle Wattles introduces is the idea of "thinking in the certain way." He states that thoughts are the starting point of all wealth creation. By thinking in a clear, focused, and purposeful way, individuals can align their desires with the universe’s abundant forces. Thoughts create mental images of success and wealth, which in turn attract the right opportunities, people, and situations.

5. Increasing Life

Wattles discusses the concept of "Increasing Life" in this chapter, stating that the purpose of acquiring wealth is to enhance life—both personally and for others. Wealth allows for the expansion of one's life experience, enabling individuals to engage more fully in their desires and contribute to the growth and betterment of others. The more we increase life for ourselves, the more we contribute to the growth and development of humanity as a whole.

6. How Riches Come to You

In this chapter, Wattles elaborates on how riches come to individuals. He stresses that wealth does not come from working harder than others or from luck but through a combination of focused thought, alignment with the natural laws of abundance, and efficient action. Wealth flows to those who are in harmony with the universe’s creative forces, who think and act in the right way.

7. Gratitude

Gratitude is a core principle in Wattles’ philosophy. He explains that expressing gratitude is one of the most powerful tools for attracting wealth. When individuals express sincere gratitude for what they have, they create an energy of positive vibration, which in turn draws more wealth and abundance into their lives. Gratitude, according to Wattles, is a necessary part of the creative process because it fosters harmony between the mind and the universe.

8. Thinking in the Certain Way

Wattles expands on the concept of thinking in the certain way. He emphasizes the need for individuals to think with clarity, focus, and positive intent. The certain way involves thinking with the expectation that wealth will come, acting as though success is already in motion. This mindset shifts the individual into alignment with the creative forces of the universe, facilitating the manifestation of wealth.

9. How to Use the Will

The will, in Wattles' philosophy, is a powerful tool for directing thoughts and actions toward achieving one's desires. This chapter discusses the importance of using willpower to stay focused and determined in pursuit of wealth. It is not about exerting forceful control but about maintaining mental discipline and making purposeful decisions aligned with one’s goals.

10. Further Use of the Will

Wattles goes deeper into the application of the will, explaining that it must be used consistently to direct the thoughts and actions toward a specific goal. Willpower is necessary to overcome distractions and obstacles. It is through persistent and disciplined use of the will that individuals are able to shape their reality and create opportunities for success and wealth.

11. Acting in the Certain Way

In this chapter, Wattles emphasizes the importance of taking action. While thought is crucial in the creation of wealth, it is the combination of correct thought and purposeful action that brings wealth into manifestation. Action must be taken with certainty and without hesitation, and it must be aligned with the mental images of success. The right actions, when taken consistently, lead to tangible results.

12. Efficient Action

Efficiency is key to success, according to Wattles. He explains that wealth is the result of effective, efficient action. One should act with purpose, focusing on the most productive tasks and not wasting energy or time on unimportant activities. By being efficient, individuals can maximize their efforts and increase their chances of success.

13. Getting into the Right Business

Wattles advises readers to get into the right business—one that aligns with their strengths, interests, and the opportunities presented by the universe. He believes that success comes when individuals find the work or business that allows them to increase life for themselves and others. By engaging in the right business, individuals can more easily attract wealth and grow their personal and financial fortunes.

14. The Impression of Increase

The concept of the “Impression of Increase” involves giving others the sense that they are becoming more, improving their lives, and moving toward greater success. By creating an atmosphere of growth and positivity around you, you attract more opportunities and wealth into your life. Wattles stresses that people should focus on being a positive influence and inspiring abundance in others to foster a similar energy for themselves.

15. The Advancing Man

In this chapter, Wattles describes the "Advancing Man" as someone who is constantly moving forward, growing, and improving. This person is focused on increasing their life and the lives of others. The advancing man understands that wealth is a byproduct of personal growth, and by continuously striving for improvement, they inevitably attract more opportunities and riches.

16. Some Cautions and Concluding Observations

Wattles concludes the book with a series of cautions. He reminds readers that the pursuit of wealth must be balanced with ethical principles. One must not harm others in their quest for riches. Instead, wealth should be acquired in ways that increase life and contribute positively to society. He emphasizes that wealth should be a force for good and that individuals should use their riches to improve the world around them.

17. Summary of The Science of Getting Rich

The final chapter provides a succinct summary of the key principles discussed throughout the book. Wattles reiterates that anyone can become rich by thinking in the right way, using the power of will, taking efficient action, and maintaining an attitude of gratitude. The science of getting rich is available to all, and by applying these principles, anyone can create wealth and abundance in their lives.

The Science of Getting Rich offers timeless wisdom and practical advice for anyone seeking to improve their financial situation and manifest abundance. By following Wattles' clear and straightforward principles, individuals can tap into the natural laws of wealth and success, ensuring that they align their thoughts, actions, and desires with the universal flow of prosperity.
