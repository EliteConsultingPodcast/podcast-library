About this book

FREEDOM FOR ALL: How to Create Wealth & Success Through Consciousness – Neville Goddard (1942).

Freedom for All by Neville Goddard is a powerful spiritual and psychological work that reveals the inner laws governing human experience. At its heart, the book teaches that true freedom does not come from changing external conditions, but from understanding and mastering the creative power within. Neville presents a bold and transformative idea: that God and man are one, and that imagination—guided by feeling and belief—is the force that shapes reality.Unlike traditional religious teachings that place God outside of man, Neville brings the concept inward. He shows that every individual possesses divine creative authority and can consciously direct their life through the disciplined use of imagination. Each chapter builds a clear and practical framework for achieving freedom—freedom from limitation, fear, lack, and undesirable conditions.Below is a detailed exploration of each chapter and its deeper meaning.

Chapter 1: The Oneness of GodNeville begins by establishing the foundational principle of the book: the oneness of God. He teaches that there is only one power, one consciousness, and one reality—God—and that this God is not separate from man but lives within him.This means that every individual is an expression of the same divine source. There are not many powers competing in the universe; there is only one creative force operating through all. Recognizing this unity eliminates the belief in external causes or forces controlling life.The realization of oneness is the beginning of freedom. When a person understands that they are not separate from God, they no longer feel powerless or dependent on outside conditions. Instead, they begin to see themselves as the operant power—the cause behind their experiences.

Chapter 2: The Name of GodIn this chapter, Neville reveals the true “name of God” as the awareness of being—I AM. This simple phrase represents the essence of existence and identity. It is the core of consciousness, the sense of being that is present in every individual.Neville explains that whatever follows “I AM” defines one’s reality. If a person says, “I am successful,” they align themselves with success. If they say, “I am unlucky,” they reinforce limitation. The name of God, therefore, is not just a title—it is a creative declaration.This chapter emphasizes the importance of self-definition. By becoming conscious of how one uses “I AM,” a person can begin to reshape their identity and, consequently, their life. The power lies not in external circumstances, but in the internal assumption of being.

Chapter 3: The Law of CreationHere, Neville introduces the law that governs all manifestation: imagination creates reality. Everything that appears in the external world is first formed in the imagination.He explains that creation is not something that happens outside of us, but through us. The images we hold in mind, combined with feeling, are projected outward as life experiences. This law operates continuously, whether one is aware of it or not.The key to using this law consciously is intention. Instead of allowing random thoughts to shape life, one must deliberately imagine what they desire. Neville encourages the reader to take control of their inner world, as it is the blueprint for the outer world.This chapter lays the groundwork for practical application, showing that creation is a mental and emotional process.
