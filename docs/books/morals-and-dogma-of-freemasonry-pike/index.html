Summary

Albert Pike's Morals and Dogma of the Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite of Freemasonry (1871) is a long series of lectures on the symbolism and philosophy behind the degrees of the Scottish Rite.

Pike moves degree by degree, from Apprentice to Sublime Prince of the Royal Secret, drawing on religion, philosophy, Kabbalah, Hermeticism and history. The lectures address duty, justice, truth, toleration, self-discipline and the pursuit of knowledge.

The podcast presents the work degree by degree, so each episode can be heard on its own. Pike's text is dense and erudite, and it is best approached as a survey of the moral and mystical ideas that Freemasonry inherited.

Key ideas

  • Each degree teaches a moral and symbolic lesson
  • Duty, justice and truth form the core
  • Ancient religions and philosophies are interconnected
  • Self-improvement is the purpose of initiation
